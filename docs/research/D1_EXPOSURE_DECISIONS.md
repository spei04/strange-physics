# D1 exposure policy decisions

Status: the owner approved the recommended policy and its source implementation
on 2026-10-31 and subsequently accepted initial evaluation custody. The actual
six-world inventory and its overlap acceptance remain pending. These decisions
do not establish inventory completeness, amend the frozen protocol bytes, or
authorize numerical execution.

The next implementation can compare supplied exposure records. It cannot infer
that those records cover every experiment or that a test catalog is an untouched
evaluation set. Before the D1 diagnostic is admitted, these gaps need an explicit
scope and a defensible disposition.

## Evidence and consequences

The bounded historical review found retained physical observations, noisy and
noiseless acceptance cases, probe material, earlier native evaluations and the
completed GRID-Q1 diagnostic. Source and fake-reference tests are also visible,
but they are different forms of exposure. The review did not establish complete
history before the earliest inspected records, external work, every hosted CI
occurrence, or an independently held six-world evaluation inventory.

Two source-level constraints prevent an honest generic inventory today:

- GRID-Q1's disjoint 64, 256 and 1,024 midpoint pools contain 1,344 numerical
  candidate coefficients before older history. The existing D1 inventory cap is
  1,024 entries. Truncation would conceal evidence.
- D1 query/history identities describe its fixed times, zero noise and small
  spring structure. Older noisy, differently sampled or broader environments
  cannot be relabeled as D1-shaped records. An incompatible query does not prove
  that its physical world is disjoint.

These are representation and provenance gaps. They are not evidence that the
eight D1 constructions overlap, nor evidence that they are unexposed.

## Recommended policy

| Decision | Recommendation | Required completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exposure roles | Retain actual observations/truth, numerically evaluated candidate/reference worlds, analytic-only vectors, and source/fake design exposure as distinct roles. Physical evaluations participate in physical overlap checks; design exposure remains visible and excludes claims of human blindness. | Role definitions and comparisons tested against actual retained inputs; no silent dropped categories. |
| Historical boundary | Freeze an exact manifest of inspected sources, runs and artifacts at the pre-admission checkpoint. Include physical qualification and CI fixtures, retain occurrence identities, and state unresolved earlier/external gaps. | Exact cutoff identities and custodian review. Unknown coverage prevents an unqualified complete-history claim. |
| Bounded representation | Add a separately reviewed bounded collection with actual-byte provenance, all-part inclusion, duplicate handling, and compatible/incompatible/unknown classifications. Preserve world-level comparison when query-level comparison is unavailable. | Schema, negative tests, source-to-record joins and fixed byte/count limits. No cap increase, pagination or compatibility projection is implemented by this proposal. |
| Reserved comparison set | Identify or deliberately establish the later six-world inventory under a named evaluation custodian before it is used. Keep its labels unavailable to development and check it against all declared physical exposures and D1 reservations. | Actual immutable inventory and overlap disposition. Empty inventories and test catalogs cannot stand in for this evidence. |
| D1 claim | Keep the eight constructions and four coefficient values development-only. Future untouched studies must exclude them and their paired variants. | Explicit exclusion in the future evaluation manifest; no generalization or human-blindness claim from D1. |

The recommended package preserves all historical evidence, leaves unresolved
coverage visible, and keeps numerical admission blocked until the protocol's
gates are met. If the intended scientific claim requires narrowing because
history cannot be reconciled, that is a separate explicit protocol decision.

## Ownership and next implementation

The repository owner is the initial evaluation custodian, following the explicit
answer, "I’ll own it initially." This assigns responsibility for the actual
reserved inventory and its overlap disposition; it does not establish an
independent reviewer, untouched labels, or an accepted six-world inventory.

Research may now implement one bounded nonnumerical extraction slice, Quality
can independently check its coverage and overlap rules, and the lead can
integrate its evidence. Actual reserved-world acceptance still needs the owner's
review of an immutable inventory and an explicit overlap disposition. This does
not authorize archive searches, new endpoints, tuning after overlap inspection,
or a scientific rerun.

Adapter, report and disabled-runner source work may continue in parallel.
The frozen D1 protocol, experiment counts, previous failed attempts and resource
ledgers remain unchanged. Policy approval is not a statement that the system
is scientifically or operationally ready.
